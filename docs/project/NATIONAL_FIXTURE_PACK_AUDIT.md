# NATIONAL first-pass fixture pack acceptance

Audited 2026-10-15 against the frozen RS-034 source list in `BACKLOG.md`. This is
fixture and offline-ingest acceptance, not live-source approval. The parser tests
check bounded identity and metadata; scan tests use `file://` inputs and the
app's normal SQLite path. Several source-specific scan tests explicitly fail if
the HTTP fetcher is called.

The additional `national_govinfo_enrolled_bills.rss` and
`openfema_declarations_2026_10_15.json` fixtures also parse and scan offline in
`test_news_ingest.py` and `test_openfema_declarations.py`.

The pack contains deliberately limited evidence. The NHC basin feed does not
establish a U.S. coastal watch. The CDC archive and notice snippets do not
establish a current alert, jurisdiction, or public-health impact. Those parser
and policy questions remain open under RS-039 and the NHC source-specific work;
their absence does not make the required local fixture families absent. All
repository defaults for these sources remain disabled unless separately admitted.
