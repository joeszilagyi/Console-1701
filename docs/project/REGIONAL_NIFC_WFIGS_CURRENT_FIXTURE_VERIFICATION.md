# NIFC WFIGS current/recent Washington incident fixture

Reviewed 2026-10-15. The [National Interagency Fire Center WFIGS description](https://data-nifc.opendata.arcgis.com/pages/d6ef1367fadc4405b5f09c98e52ed972) says its Wildland Fire Incident Locations service represents current or recent incidents and is working data derived from IRWIN. Its [public incident layer](https://services3.arcgis.com/T4QMspbfLg3qTGWY/arcgis/rest/services/WFIGS_Incident_Locations_Current/FeatureServer/0) exposes `UniqueFireIdentifier`, `IrwinID`, name, Washington point-of-origin state/county, discovery and modification times, acreage, containment, and `ActiveFireCandidate`.

A bounded exact [ArcGIS query](https://services3.arcgis.com/T4QMspbfLg3qTGWY/arcgis/rest/services/WFIGS_Incident_Locations_Current/FeatureServer/0/query) selected `POOState='US-WA'`, wildfire category `WF`, `ActiveFireCandidate=1`, at least 1,000 acres, and modification on or after 2026-10-01. It requested 17 metadata fields, no geometry, ordered by modification time, and capped at 25 rows. The response returned HTTP 200, 15 features, no transfer-limit flag, and 10,367 bytes. A reduced metadata copy is checked in as `tests/fixtures/news/regional_nifc_wfigs_current_wa.json` (12,009 formatted bytes). Sisi and Little Giant have `UniqueFireIdentifier` values matching the already observed NIFC IMSR records and NWCC normalized keys.

The source's `ActiveFireCandidate=1` is **not** a verified active-incident status. Sinlahekin, Kaiser Canyon, Modrite, Second Street, Hatch Grade, Hudspeth, and Bradeen Hill were all reported at 100% containment in this selected response. The parser stores the flag as source evidence while setting `active_fire_claimed=false`, `evacuation_claimed=false`, and `public_impact_inferred=false`. It does not retain geometry or incident commander data. The three NIFC/NWCC views share one interagency family, so their exact-ID deduplication does not count as independent corroboration.

The registry entry defaults off and the parser accepts only an explicit local fixture with the exact observed query as `official_url`. It rejects malformed identity, foreign state, non-wildfire type, changed candidate flag, contradictory dates, oversized or truncated responses, and duplicate fire IDs. The offline scan traps HTTP and confirms a live-configured source is policy-blocked. A future live path needs a rolling bounded time window, volume/cadence review, exact-source allowlist and separate signoff. The WA DNR active-incident source remains a distinct unresolved requirement.
