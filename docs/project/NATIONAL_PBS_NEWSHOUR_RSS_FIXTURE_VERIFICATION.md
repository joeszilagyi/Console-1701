# PBS NewsHour RSS NATIONAL fixture verification

Checked 2026-10-15 against the design's exact [PBS NewsHour headlines RSS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/feeds/rss/headlines). A bounded GET returned HTTP 200, `application/rss+xml`, a 36,859-byte RSS 2.0 document, and 20 items. The channel identified itself as `PBS News Hour - The Latest` with `https://www.pbs.org/newshour/` as its link. Items included title, PBS article URL, publication date, permalink GUID, description, optional categories, and optional Dublin Core creator. Some creators named Associated Press; a PBS feed item is therefore not evidence of independent PBS reporting. Response headers included `ETag`, `Last-Modified`, and a five-minute cache period. These observations verify a fixture shape, not live reuse permission or a suitable polling cadence.

The fixture parser requires the exact registered NATIONAL feed identity and channel, caps payloads at 128 KiB and snapshots at 50 entries, rejects DTD/entity declarations, and retains only `/newshour/nation/` article paths. It stores title, official publisher link/permalink, publication time, bounded creator, and source/section evidence. Descriptions, category lists, media attachments, and article bodies are discarded. The nation section does not prove national impact, an event type, original reporting, or independent corroboration; stored NATIONAL items remain background absent later source-backed evidence.

The registry entry remains disabled by default and the publisher feed is absent from the app's exact official HTTP allowlist. Offline scan tests trap HTTP. Before any live admission, review PBS reuse policy and rate terms, source freshness, retention, and a separate non-government publisher transport policy; this first-pass fixture does not authorize such a change.
